# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome here, whatever their background, identity or experience.

- Be kind and patient. Many people arrive here because their PC is broken and they're stressed.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Disagree with reasons, and accept a maintainer's decision.
- No harassment, insults, sexual content or personal attacks, in any form or place connected to this project.
- Don't share other people's private data, links, keys or files.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem privately, use **Report a vulnerability** on the Security tab, or contact a maintainer through GitHub.
